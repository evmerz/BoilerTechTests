About Us!

We are a team of four computer science students studying at Purdue University. Our goal is to help other students excel in computer science with a curated selection of quizzes and coding questions!

To start studying as a guest, visit the guest page using the navigation bar! If you wish to save your progress, you can make at account with us by clicking the profile icon.

Please note that our website is still in development, so some features may be missing or have minor bugs. Thank you for your support!
